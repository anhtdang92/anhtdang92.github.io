How ATLAS works

The end-to-end ML pipeline behind ATLAS, my stock-return forecasting project — from raw prices to a served, risk-controlled forecast.
  1. DataDaily OHLCV prices for a 33-stock universe (tech, sector leaders, defensives, ETFs, growth), with two years of history per symbol for training.Yahoo Finance (yfinance) · BigQuery (optional)
  2. Features34 technical indicators (moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, volume, momentum, volatility, ATR, regime, calendar), built into 30-day lookback windows.Pandera schemas validate prices, features and predictions
  3. ModelsA 2-layer LSTM (Huber loss, MC Dropout uncertainty) in a 7-architecture ablation: LSTM, BiLSTM+Attention, Transformer, CNN-LSTM, Multi-Horizon, XGBoost, Ridge.TensorFlow/Keras · XGBoost · scikit-learn
  4. EvaluationWalk-forward cross-validation, Bayesian tuning, bootstrap confidence intervals (10K resamples) and Diebold-Mariano tests against baselines.Optuna · SHAP · MLflow
  5. ServingA REST endpoint at /api/v1/predict. Vertex AI first, then the local model, then an explicit failure — no silent mock fallback.FastAPI · Vertex AI · Docker Compose
  6. Portfolio21-day forecasts feed a rebalancer with risk controls (10% max position, 8% stop-loss, 12% drawdown circuit breaker) in paper-trading mode.Streamlit dashboard · Paper trading
